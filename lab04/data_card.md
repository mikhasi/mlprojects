# UCI Adult Income Dataset

## Dataset Overview
* **Source:** Extracted by Barry Becker from the 1994 U.S. Census Bureau database
* **License:** Open Database License (ODbL) / Public Domain attribution via OpenML.
* **Size:** 
  * Raw Rows: 48,842
  * Raw Columns: 15 attributes.

## Biases and Limitations
* **Temporal Bias:** The data reflects economic, social, and demographic information from **1994**.
* **Demographic Bias:** Inequalities exist across racial and gender groups due to historical systemic biases present in 1990s census collection.

## Privacy & Ethical Concerns
* **Anonymization:** While names and social security numbers have been removed, Some labels like (`age`, `race`, `sex`, `native-country`, `education`) can potentially be linked to specific individuals.
